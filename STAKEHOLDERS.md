# Stakeholders: Campus Buddy Platform

This document identifies the groups and individuals who have a vested interest in the development, deployment, and success of the **Campus Buddy** platform.

---

## 👥 1. Primary Stakeholders (Direct Users)
These individuals interact with the platform daily and are the main target of its functionalities.

*   **Current Students**:
    *   *Interest*: Academic organization, routine tracking, accessing notes/question banks, and social networking with peers.
    *   *Impact*: Improved academic efficiency and stronger campus community connection.
*   **University Alumni**:
    *   *Interest*: Maintaining connection with their alma mater, providing mentorship, and talent discovery.
    *   *Impact*: Increased professional networking opportunities and career development support.
*   **System Administrators**:
    *   *Interest*: Platform stability, data integrity, and content moderation.
    *   *Impact*: Ensures a safe, clean, and professional environment for all users.

---

## 🏛 2. Secondary Stakeholders (Indirectly Affected)
Individuals who may not use the platform every day but benefit from its existence.

*   **Faculty & Department Heads**:
    *   *Interest*: Streamlined dissemination of academic resources and better student organization.
    *   *Impact*: Reduced administrative burden for answering repetitive questions about schedules or exam papers.
*   **Campus Club Leaders**:
    *   *Interest*: Increasing visibility for club events and recruiting new members.
    *   *Impact*: Higher engagement in extracurricular activities.
*   **University Management/Registrar**:
    *   *Interest*: Student retention, engagement metrics, and alumni-university relations.
    *   *Impact*: Strengthened overall institutional reputation.

---

## 🛠 3. Technical & Internal Stakeholders
The individuals responsible for the platform's creation and upkeep.

*   **Project Developers**:
    *   *Interest*: Code quality, technical scalability, and successful project delivery for academic or professional credit.
    *   *Impact*: Direct control over the platform's features and performance.
*   **IT Infrastructure Providers**:
    *   *Interest*: Efficient server resource usage and security compliance.
    *   *Impact*: Critical for the platform's uptime and data protection.

---

## 💼 4. External Stakeholders
External entities that might interact with the outcome of the platform's data or users.

*   **Recruiters & Industry Partners**:
    *   *Interest*: Finding skilled students and graduates through the "Talent Showcase" and "Alumni Hub."
    *   *Impact*: Bridge between academia and the professional industry.
*   **Parents & Guardians**:
    *   *Interest*: Ensuring students have the right tools for academic success.
    *   *Impact*: Indirect reassurance of the university’s support systems.

---

| Stakeholder | Influence | Interest | Key Responsibility/Requirement |
